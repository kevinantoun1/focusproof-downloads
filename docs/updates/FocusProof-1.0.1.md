# FocusProof 1.0.1

Version 1.0.1 (build 6) · 5 August 2026 · Stable · macOS 15 or later

A small maintenance release. Nothing about how sessions are recorded, timed or
calculated has changed: Study Time, Focus Score, Score Coverage, away-study
accounting, backups and Study Receipts all behave exactly as they did in 1.0.0.

## What changed

- **A proper installation window.** Opening the disk image now shows FocusProof
  and your Applications folder side by side, with an arrow between them and a
  single instruction, on a FocusProof background. Previously the image opened as
  a plain folder listing.
- **An optional support link.** Settings → About now has a Support section with a
  link to the project's Ko-fi page. It is a link and nothing more — it opens in
  your browser, appears nowhere else in the app, never interrupts a session, and
  changes nothing about how FocusProof works whether or not you use it.

## Independent distribution

FocusProof is distributed independently and is not notarized by Apple. macOS
requires manual approval the first time you open it. Download the disk image,
drag FocusProof to Applications, try to open it, then open
**System Settings → Privacy & Security**, choose **Open Anyway** for FocusProof,
and confirm **Open**.

FocusProof does not claim Apple approval, notarization, Developer ID signing or
Gatekeeper approval.

## Privacy

Unchanged. Your study data stays on your Mac unless you choose to export or
share it. FocusProof records only during a session you start, and never records
page contents, full addresses, keystrokes, screenshots or your clipboard.

## Updating

If you already have FocusProof 1.0.0, Check for Updates will offer this release.
Your sessions, settings and classifications are preserved.
